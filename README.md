# My Favorite Series: Lupin

**Lupin** is a French mystery thriller series inspired by the adventures of the gentleman thief *Arsène Lupin*. It is full of clever heists, suspense, and unexpected twists.

---

## Main Character

- **Assane Diop** – The protagonist, a master thief who seeks justice for his father’s wrongful imprisonment.
- **Claire** – Assane’s love interest.
- **Julien** – Assane’s friend who helps him in tricky situations.

---

## Why I Love Lupin

1. Smart and clever heists.
2. Suspenseful and unpredictable plot.
3. Amazing character development.
4. Stylish cinematography and soundtrack.

---

### Favorite Quotes

> "The world belongs to those who dare to dream." – Assane Diop

> "A gentleman thief is always polite." – Arsène Lupin

### Seasons

- **Season 1** – Introduction to Assane’s story and first big heist.  
- **Season 2** – Continuation of Assane’s revenge and more thrilling plans.
